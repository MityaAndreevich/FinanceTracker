# Budget Crab — Launch Promotion + Budget Plan (first push)

Every number traces to our research (launch benchmarks, positioning R2, pricing). Where a number is an explicit estimate, it's flagged. Current pricing = Path A: **$4.99/mo · $34.99/yr (30-day trial) · $99.99 lifetime**, hard paywall, Quiet-Premium positioning.

## 1. Reality anchor (so the budget is sane, not hopeful)
- **Month-1 installs, solo indie, no audience: p50 = 200–450.** 65–75% of solo apps NEVER exceed 300 in M1.
- **Funnel at $4.99 (p50, ~300 installs):** ~6–8% install→trial = ~21 trials → 25–35% trial→paid = ~6 paid → **~$275 M1 revenue** (p75 ~1,200 installs → ~$800).
- **Time to $1K MRR (Finance): ~100 days.** Only **17.3%** of subscription apps ever reach $1K MRR; **57.7%** never cross $1K *lifetime*. Median 1-yr-old indie app = **$72/mo**.
- **Blunt implication:** paid acquisition is **negative-EV in month 1**. CPI is $2–10; a paid user is worth ~$35/yr and only ~2–6% of installs convert. Buying 1,000 ASA installs at $5 CPI = **$5,000 spent to earn ~$525–980**. So the first push is **mostly free channels**; paid money is for *learning*, not growth, until the funnel is proven.

## 2. Pre-push checklist (FREE, highest leverage — do before spending a cent)
These move conversion far more than ad dollars at our stage. Most already have briefs (see index).
- **ASO metadata** — keyword-first title + subtitle; own low-competition long-tails we already satisfy: `CSV export`, `private budget`, `offline budget`, `manual expense tracker`, `no bank link`, `bill reminder`. (Finance revenue +10.2% YoY → users pay for value, not cheapest.)
- **Screenshots** — 6.9" set, billboard style, ≥40pt top-left caption (Apple OCR indexes it): frame 1 = "100% Private · Local Only". (BRIEF_26B.)
- **App Preview video** — 15–30s vertical, autoplay-muted, shows Quick Add + Safe-to-Spend. (BRIEF_27; not a launch blocker but lifts conversion.)
- **Apple "aha" in 60 seconds** — 55% of trial cancels happen Day 0. Onboarding demo data must show value instantly (this ties to the v1.0.1 analytics redesign — no blank state).
- **Editorial featuring nomination** — 1-page pitch, free, Quiet-Premium + privacy angle Apple editors favor. (BRIEF_26C.)

## 4. Budget options (pick one)
**Option A — Lean / $0–100 (RECOMMENDED for launch).**
Free channels only + optional $50 one-day ASA velocity nudge on launch day. Rationale: our funnel isn't yet validated; spend money *after* you have retention/conversion data. Best risk-adjusted EV at p50.

**Option B — Learn / ~$300–500.**
Free channels + **ASA $50/day × 5–7 days** ($250–350) as a controlled experiment to measure real CPI and install→trial, + ~$100 reserve for a screenshot/caption A/B. You're buying *data*, not users. Reasonable if you want the velocity signal + learning and can treat the spend as tuition.

**Option C — Aggressive / ~$1,500.**
ASA $100/day × 14 days. **I'd advise against this now** — at our conversion stage it's likely a ~3–5× loss and you can't yet A/B fast enough to fix the funnel mid-flight (experimentation velocity is the real multiplier: 18.7–40× revenue premium — but that's *tests*, not spend). Revisit once D7/trial-conversion data clears p50.

## 5. My recommendation (honest)
**Go Option A for the actual launch, hold Option B as a fast-follow.** Sequence:
1. Ship the pre-push free checklist (§2) — this is where the conversion gains are.
2. Launch Tuesday: Product Hunt + Reddit + optional $50 ASA day-1 burst for velocity.
3. Watch **install→trial, D1/D7 retention, trial→paid** for 2–4 weeks against p50 benchmarks below.
4. **Only if the funnel clears p50**, deploy Option B's ASA experiment to learn CPI, then scale paid **only** where LTV > CPI. Until then, every dollar is better spent on ASO + assets + A/B tests.

Money is not the lever at 300 installs — **conversion and retention are.** Buying installs into an unproven funnel just pays Apple to expose a leak.

## 6. Success metrics & kill criteria (p50 baseline / stretch)
| Metric | Baseline (p50) | Stretch |
|---|---|---|
| Install→trial | 6–8% | 9–10% |
| Trial→paid | 25–35% | 40–45% |
| D1 / D7 / D30 retention | 35–45 / 15–25 / 8–15% | 50+ / 25–35 / 12–18% |
| Rating | 4.0 floor, 4.5 target | 4.5+ |
| $1K MRR | ~100 days | 60–90 days |

**Kill/scale rule:** if after ~2 weeks install→trial < ~4% or Day-0 trial cancels are extreme, the leak is onboarding/paywall — fix that before any paid spend. If it clears p50, ASA scaling becomes defensible.
